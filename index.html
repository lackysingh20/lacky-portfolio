About Us

Who Am I?

Hi, I'm Lacky Singh. I live in Mumbai, where I spend my days convincing large language models to behave like responsible adults. Officially I am an AI Engineer with 4+ years of building production GenAI systems: multi-agent pipelines, RAG, text-to-SQL agents and data lakes, for clients in finance, pharma, manufacturing, metals and logistics.

At AGR Knowledge Services I joined as AI Engineer (SDE 1) in March 2024, levelled up to SDE 2 in July 2024 and SDE 3 in April 2025, which is roughly one boss fight per year. Along the way I built a 10-agent tender platform that runs a nine-stage bid lifecycle (the agents, mercifully, still ask a human before doing anything important), an analytics agent that cut data requests by 80% so colleagues now ask the warehouse instead of me, a procurement copilot that turned 15 days of negotiation prep into 1, and a market data lake for Nuvama Wealth that made backtests 90% faster.

Before that I kept servers alive at SupportHives while finishing my B.Sc. and MCA, which taught me that sleep is a scheduling problem. I like owning things end to end, from the PRD to the production deploy, so product roles interest me as much as engineering ones. Off the clock: content writing, nature walks, surfing the web for far too long, and volunteering, which I did as a Manager of DLLE (Department of Lifelong Learning and Extension) in college.
